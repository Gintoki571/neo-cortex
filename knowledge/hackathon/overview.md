# Tracks

Three tracks. You may enter one, two or all three, and you must name every track you enter at submission.[^hackathon-page][^rules-page]

> "Agents That Act on Connected Data", "Agent Memory and Coordination", "Company Brain"
> Evidence: [wemakedevs-falkordb-hackathon.txt](/references/evidence/hackathon/wemakedevs-falkordb-hackathon.txt)

Per-track detail and the prize for each track are in [judging-and-prizes](/hackathon/judging-and-prizes.md).

# Eligibility and team

- Open to developers worldwide: "The hackathon is open to developers worldwide."[^hackathon-page]
- Team size: "You can participate solo or in a team of up to 4 members. Each participant may only be part of one team."[^rules-page]
- No graph database experience required: "No. The getting started guide and the kickoff session cover the basics you need to begin."[^hackathon-page]
- Age, country or residency limits: not stated.
