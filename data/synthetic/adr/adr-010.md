# ADR-010: Lift the Friday deploy freeze

## Status

Accepted, 2026-09-22. Supersedes ADR-004.

## Context

ADR-004 stopped production deploys on Fridays. In practice the freeze held back fixes that were ready by Friday afternoon, and those fixes then rushed out on Monday with less review time. Some of the delay was simply the cost of waiting, and some of it pushed teams toward larger batches of change that were harder to roll back.

The canary tooling has matured since the freeze was introduced. We can now send a release to a small slice of traffic, watch it, and stop it quickly, which was not realistic when ADR-004 was written.

## Decision

Friday production deploys are allowed before 14:00 UTC, and every Friday deploy goes out behind a canary. Friday deploys after 14:00 UTC are not allowed. This record lifts the freeze and replaces ADR-004 entirely.

## Consequences

- A fix that is ready on a Friday morning can ship the same day.
- The on-call team watches Friday canaries with the same care it gives any other release.
- Teams should avoid bundling large changes into a Friday morning deploy, since a canary is only as good as the change it protects.
- References to the old freeze in other documents are out of date and should be corrected as they are found.
- Mateo reports on Friday deploy outcomes at the next on-call handover, so the decision can be checked against what actually happened.
