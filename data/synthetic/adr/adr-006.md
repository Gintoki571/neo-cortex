# ADR-006: Platform owns shared stateful infrastructure

## Status

Accepted, 2026-08-11. session-cache moves from Identity to Platform effective 2026-09-01.

## Context

Identity has been carrying a lot of recent operational load, and the shared stateful pieces it runs sit awkwardly next to its product work. Engineers who should be building login features have been spending a large share of their time on operating shared stores. Ravi and Grace both raised this with me separately, and they describe the same problem from their two sides.

Ownership of shared state should sit with the team whose job is operating shared infrastructure, and that team is Platform. The question is not about any one incident. It is about where the work should live so that the people doing it can get good at it.

## Decision

Platform owns shared stateful infrastructure. session-cache moves from Identity to Platform effective 2026-09-01. Identity keeps the services that sit on top of it and continues to own its own product behaviour.

## Consequences

- The session-cache owner changes on 1 September 2026, and handover notes are due before then.
- Runbooks and dashboards for session-cache move with the ownership.
- Identity engineers will go through Platform for changes to shared state, and Platform will ask Identity for input on anything that affects logins.
- The ownership table in the engineering handbook is updated when the move takes effect.
- Identity and Platform run a joint handover session in the last week of August, so that the open session-cache work is not lost in the move.
