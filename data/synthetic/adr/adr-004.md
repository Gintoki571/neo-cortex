# ADR-004: No production deploys on Fridays

## Status

Accepted, 2026-06-30. Effective 2026-07-03.

## Context

INC-05 came from a Friday-evening deploy. The release went out late in the day, and the problem it carried could only be fixed over the weekend, when the people who knew the code were mostly offline. Tessa Ngu did the fix, but a fix made on a weekend is always more fragile than one made on a weekday with the whole team around.

I have been on call for long enough to see this pattern more than once. A Friday deploy has a longer tail than any other deploy, because nobody wants to be the person who gets paged at night for a change they shipped on the way out the door.

## Decision

No production deploys happen on Fridays. The rule takes effect on 3 July 2026. Non-production work on a Friday is unaffected.

## Consequences

- Fixes that become ready on a Friday wait until Monday unless they are urgent.
- An urgent hotfix on a Friday needs an exception from the CTO before anyone touches production.
- The on-call team plans Friday changes for the next working day, so the handover is clean.
- Existing deploy documentation that does not mention this rule will be updated as it is next touched.
- If the freeze starts to hold back fixes that customers are waiting for, the on-call team raises it at the next leadership sync rather than working around it quietly.
