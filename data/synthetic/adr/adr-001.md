# ADR-001: Remember this device (30-day device trust)

## Status

Accepted, 2026-06-04.

## Context

Enterprise customers have been telling us that they are asked for a second factor every single day, sometimes several times in one working session. Kilnworth Dental Group raised it most loudly, because their clinic staff share machines and sign in constantly. Support is handling a steady stream of these tickets, and the MFA prompt has become the most common reason a customer says the login feels broken.

Anjali and I looked at the options. Asking less often is cheap for us and removes most of the friction, provided the trust is scoped to a single device and expires on its own. Noor and Lukas went through the identity-side impact with us, and their notes are attached to the working ticket.

## Decision

Adopt "remember this device". After a successful second-factor check, the device is trusted for 30 days, and the user is not prompted again on that device during that window. The trust belongs to the device, not to the customer account, so a new laptop still prompts.

## Consequences

- Users on a trusted device skip the repeated MFA prompt for 30 days.
- Each login now stores an extra piece of state about the device. Anyone touching the login path should keep that in mind when they reason about load.
- Support gets a short canned reply for users who ask why a new laptop still prompts, and the same reply covers the 30-day window.
- We will revisit the window length once the first quarter of data is in.
