# ADR-005: Rename bankbridge to bank-connector

## Status

Accepted, 2026-07-17. Effective 2026-07-20.

## Context

The service that talks to our banking partners is called bankbridge. The name has started to cause trouble in two places. The test mock we run in CI is called bankbridge-sim, and in logs and dashboards it is easy to mistake the mock for the real connector. The name also breaks the `<noun>-connector` pattern that the rest of our services follow, so new engineers keep asking whether it is the same kind of thing.

I raised this after a week of confusion in the payments channel. Daniel and I agreed the cost of a rename is small now, and it only gets larger as more references pile up.

## Decision

Rename bankbridge to bank-connector, effective 2026-07-20. The migration checklist covers code, dashboards and docs. Sofia Marchetti made the decision, and Tobias Wren approved it.

## Consequences

- Deploys, imports and dashboards move to the new name on 20 July.
- The bankbridge-sim mock keeps its name for now, so people will need to recognise both names for a while.
- Old references in documents will read as stale until someone touches them.
- Anyone who searches logs for the old name should search for the new one from 20 July onwards.
- Release notes for the rename say so plainly, so that anyone reading older tickets can match the two names.
