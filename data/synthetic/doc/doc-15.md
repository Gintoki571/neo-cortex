# Customer account overview

This sheet lists our six active customers, what each one uses us for and who on our side looks after them. Keep it current when a contact changes. Figures are as of June 2026.

## The accounts

**Orchard & Vane** is a retail chain on our Enterprise plan. It is our heaviest user of the payment API, so any payment-path problem reaches them first. Maggie Ellison is the executive sponsor for the account.

**Northgate Freight** is a logistics firm. It depends on our reporting for month-end invoice totals, and its finance team reads those totals directly. Anjali Deshpande is the day-to-day contact.

**Kilnworth Dental Group** runs nine clinics and has a large number of people using the web dashboard every day. Jonas Albrecht is the support contact, and clinic managers should go to him first.

**Saltmarsh Foods** is a food distributor that pays its suppliers through our payment API. Jonas Albrecht handles support for them as well.

**Tidewater Credit Union** sends ACH through bankbridge. It has a same-day cutoff override of 17:30 ET, which is later than our default, and the account needs that setting to stay exactly as agreed.

**Bluepeak Logistics** uses our CSV exports from reporting-service. It is a separate account from Northgate Freight, even though the two names are similar, so check the account ID before replying to either.

## Account health

Kilnworth and Orchard & Vane are the two accounts where a day of trouble costs the most goodwill. Keep their contacts informed when something is under investigation, even before there is a fix.

## Contacts

Support questions go to the support channel. Commercial questions go to Anjali. Anything involving money moving goes to Payments through the normal route, not through the account contact.
